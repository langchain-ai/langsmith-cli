## This template: experiment-comparison dashboard

Compares evaluation experiments for one dataset — regressions in feedback
scores, latency, cost, and tokens vs a chosen baseline. See `src/api.ts` for
the calls it makes and `src/lib/delta.ts` for how deltas are computed.

This is just a starting point, not a spec. Change the metrics, the charts,
or the whole concept — rip out anything here and build whatever app you
actually want.
